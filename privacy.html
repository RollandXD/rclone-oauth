Privacy Policy

rclone-arch is a personal OAuth client used only by its owner with the open-source tool rclone.

  • Google Drive data is accessed only on the owner's own computer, to sync the owner's own files.
  • No data is collected, stored on any server, shared, or sold to third parties.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

隐私政策

本客户端仅供所有者本人配合 rclone 使用：只在本人电脑上访问本人的 Google Drive 文件，不收集、不上传到任何服务器、不与第三方共享或出售任何数据。可随时在 Google 账号权限页撤销授权。
